Use the V4 assets in `FanPass_VI_Redesign_V4/` for all future UI/UX implementation. Earlier assets in this folder are historical drafts and must not be used as the visual source for new work.

## Usage Rules

- Do not redesign the logo, mark, or app icon.
- Do not add decorative diagonal lines behind or across content.
- Do not place NFC waves behind important text, QR codes, prices, buttons, charts, or logos.
- Use V4 colors and gradients only.
- Keep the UI premium, trustworthy, global, and iOS-native.
- Avoid crypto-heavy consumer-facing language.

## Archived Historical Assets

Files such as `fanpass-logo-mark.svg`, `fanpass-wordmark.svg`, `fanpass-horizontal-lockup.svg`, `fanpass-app-icon.svg`, and related non-V4 drafts were created before Clean V4 arrived. They are archived under `assets/archive/brand-v0/` and must not be used as active UI or brand assets.
