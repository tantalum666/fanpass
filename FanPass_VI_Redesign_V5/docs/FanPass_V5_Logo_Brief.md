# FanPass V5 Logo Brief

Status: Round 06 Inter 700 logo lockup after owner selected option 3.
Date: 2026-06-29.
Design source: `docs/design.md`.

## Brand

Name: FanPass.

Category: live-event discovery, ticketing, secure entry pass, and controlled resale.

Audience: young live-event fans who want culture and convenience, plus organizers and staff who need verified admission.

## Direction

V5 identity direction: `Backstage Pass`.

Mix:

- 65% live culture.
- 35% premium pass/wallet.

The logo must be young and current, but not loud. It should feel simple, high-end, and trustworthy.

## Must Avoid

- Solana-like striping.
- Cyan/violet/magenta crypto gradients.
- Chain links.
- Shields.
- Coins.
- Token nodes.
- QR fragments.
- NFC waves.
- Lightning-bolt tech marks.
- Generic checkmarks.
- Nightclub flyer chaos.

## Required Tests

Each candidate route should work as:

- black on white;
- white on black;
- square app icon crop;
- horizontal wordmark lockup;
- 24px small-size mark.

Generated typography is directional only. Final production will require deterministic vector construction, optical correction, small-size testing, and trademark review.

## Routes

Round 02 shifted the logo work from mark-first to typography-first. Round 03 custom drawing was rejected as too cartoon-like. Round 04 compared OFL commercial-use fonts. The owner selected option 3 from that board: `Inter 700`. Round 06 now pairs Inter 700 with a restrained no-letter pass-notch mark.

### Current Typography Routes

1. `Humanist Premium`: modern humanist sans, `FanPass`, current owner-preferred and recommended baseline.
2. `Lowercase Culture`: younger `fanpass`, useful for energy but may lose authority.
3. `Editorial Pass`: high-end serif, distinctive but can drift too fashion/magazine.
4. `Venue Ticket`: uppercase venue/ticket voice, strong but can become mechanical.
5. `Soft Humanist`: warmer and friendlier, but may be too soft for checkout/payment.
6. `Pass Label`: split product emphasis, clear but too literal as a main logo.

### Rejected Round 01 Mark Routes

The owner rejected the first three logo directions, especially their typography. These remain archived for reference only and should not be treated as active recommendations.

### 1. Folded Pass F

Family: monogram / pass metaphor.

Concept: A compact `F` built from a folded event pass edge and one entry notch. It should be recognizable without literal ticket clip art.

Best for: App icon, tab bar, compact navigation, official ticket label.

Watch out: Do not let it become a fintech monogram or a crypto wallet icon.

### 2. Stage Aperture

Family: abstract symbol / venue metaphor.

Concept: A rectangular stage doorway or aperture of light, suggesting discovering and entering a live event.

Best for: Brand mark, launch screen, app icon, event discovery moments.

Watch out: Do not make it look like a generic camera, portal, or tech startup mark.

### 3. Entry Stamp

Family: stamp / utility mark.

Concept: A modern official-entry stamp with one decisive cut or notch. More verified pass than club stamp.

Best for: Ticket verification, secure resale, entry-pass surfaces.

Watch out: Do not make it feel bureaucratic, government, or luxury seal.

## First Exploration Prompt

Create a logo exploration board for FanPass. Show three distinct logo routes on a clean white inspection background: Folded Pass F, Stage Aperture, Entry Stamp. Each route should include a black-on-white mark, a white-on-black mark, a square app icon crop, and a simple horizontal wordmark lockup reading FanPass. Style: 65% live-culture ticketing app, 35% premium pass/wallet product. Young, simple, premium, editorial, trustworthy. Use warm paper, ink black, restrained signal red, subtle brass, and optional cobalt. Avoid neon, cyan-violet-magenta gradients, crypto symbols, chain links, shields, coins, QR fragments, NFC waves, lightning marks, betting/trading aesthetics, and nightclub flyer chaos. Vector-friendly, crisp geometry, generous spacing, no taglines.
